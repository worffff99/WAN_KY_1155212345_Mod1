1992 Tung Chung Valley Archives

With the Rose Garden Project — the building of the new airport at Chek Lap Kok — under way, the development of Tung Chung New Town was also placed on the agenda, and the Government commissioned the Department of Anthropology of The Chinese University of Hong Kong to carry out large-scale ethnographic research in the Tung Chung Valley around 1992.
